I

Purpose

Evidence before exposure.

Cinque Port examines the space between claim and commitment: where uncertainty delays a transaction, exposes capital, or forces somebody to rely on trust when evidence would be better.

Our interest is practical. Who is the party? Do they have authority? Does the underlying transaction exist? Can the claim be independently established?

II

Our origins

Born from an evidence-led approach.

Cinque Port grew out of the intelligence-led approach developed within Pavilion (Stockport) Ltd.

Pavilion’s work in the construction sector increasingly demonstrated the value of looking beyond the information immediately presented: researching underlying evidence, testing assumptions, identifying inconsistencies and distinguishing what can be established from what cannot.

That approach revealed a wider commercial problem. Organisations across industries are asked to commit money, goods or credit while material facts remain uncertain. Cinque Port was established as Pavilion’s specialist Trade Intelligence & Verification division to apply that evidence-led approach beyond Pavilion’s core construction-sector activities.

Our work

Independent evidence for commercial decisions.

Establish the material facts before value changes hands.

Cinque Port undertakes bounded commercial intelligence, evidence research and verification where unresolved uncertainty may delay or prevent a transaction. The commissioning party retains the commercial, credit and funding decision.

01 · IdentityWho is this party?
02 · AuthorityCan they act for whom they claim?
03 · ControlDo they control the relevant asset, account or channel?
04 · ExistenceDoes the transaction or receivable exist as claimed?
05 · CapabilityCan they actually perform?
Findings are evidence, not legal, regulatory, financial or compliance approval.

Operating principles

Old standards.
Modern evidence.

01

No invented certainty.

If a fact cannot be established, we say so.

02

Evidence before assertion.

A claim is kept separate from what can actually be demonstrated.

03

Answer the question.

Specific commercial questions deserve specific findings, not decorative scores.

04

Follow the consequence.

Verification matters because of the decision, capital or transaction that depends upon it.
